# SLD SGS-flux diagnostic audit — 2026-09-21 UTC

Read-only audit of the GPU-admitted `9fb39dc/02a1647` core and existing GABLS1 four-case exports. No run, immutable source, or export was changed. This supersedes the SLD public-persistence staging plan for flux/depth products until corrected diagnostics are available.

## Cause, with pinned source locations

The GABLS1 writer merges frozen `GABLSDiagnostics.build_gabls_diagnostics` with SLD coefficient/guard outputs in `source/BreezeEvaluation.jl/cases/surface_layer/diagnostics/GABLS1SurfaceLayerDiagnostics.jl:22-27`. The frozen helper at `source/BreezeEvaluation.jl/source_snapshots/gabls1/original/source/examples/gabls_diagnostics.jl:64-96` *does* enter the non-`Nothing` closure method, but calls `Jᶜz`, `𝒯_uz`, and `𝒯_vz` with `time_discretization(closure)`. SLD defaults to `VerticallyImplicitTimeDiscretization` at `source/Breeze.jl/src/TurbulenceClosures/surface_layer_diffusivity.jl:56-106`, with nonzero `Kᵘ` and scalar diffusivities exposed at lines 306-314.

Pinned Oceananigans 0.113.0 (`~/.julia/packages/Oceananigans/Kal90/src/TurbulenceClosures/abstract_scalar_diffusivity_closure.jl:270-279,289-310`) intentionally elides the implicit interior vertical flux from the *explicit tendency*: `ivd_viscous_flux_uz/vz(::AVD)=zero`, `diffusive_flux_z(::VITD)=zero` away from bounded-grid endpoints. The full constitutive vertical flux is instead `-νz ∂z u`, `-νz ∂z v`, or `-κz ∂z scalar` (same file:249-262); its effect is handled by the implicit solve, not the explicit tendency. Breeze's `𝒯_uz`, `𝒯_vz`, and `Jᶜz` wrappers call these Oceananigans functions (`source/Breeze.jl/src/TurbulenceClosures/TurbulenceClosures.jl:68-70,84-91`). Thus the frozen writer recorded an explicit-tendency residual as if it were the full physical SGS flux. This is a diagnostic error, not evidence that the simulated SLD closure was inactive.

GABLS3 has the same defect: `source/BreezeEvaluation.jl/cases/gabls3/diagnostics/GABLS3Diagnostics.jl:39-46,135-154,215-222` loads the same frozen helper for momentum/heat and calls its same scalar SGS path for moisture. The GABLS3 no-closure control correctly has zero interior SGS flux; its three SLD variants do not. No source-specific dispatch bypasses this issue.

Direct exported evidence: in each of the three GABLS1 SLD final-hour comparison profiles, `sgs_u_w_flux`, `sgs_v_w_flux`, and `sgs_w_theta_flux` are identically zero on all 33 faces, yet `surface_layer_viscosity` at z=12.5 m is respectively 1.3151, 1.3214, and 1.4554 m²/s; the two-face case also has 1.2181 m²/s at z=25 m. The exported `total_u_w_flux` equals the resolved flux at these interior faces and substitutes the actual wall flux at z=0; e.g. the two-face final hour is -0.07535 at z=0 but -0.00373 at z=12.5 m. Those are not full stress profiles. The no-closure control's SGS=0 is correct.

## Affected versus retained products

For **SLD cases only**, withhold or mark invalid `sgs_u_w_flux`, `sgs_v_w_flux`, `sgs_w_theta_flux` and GABLS3 `sgs_w_q_flux`; their `total_*` counterparts; `sgs_buoyancy_flux` and `total_buoyancy_flux`; `total_stress_magnitude`; SGS/total shear and buoyancy TKE production derived from those fluxes; and the 60-second `stress_height_0_05`, `boundary_layer_height`, and `boundary_layer_height_valid` series. A validity flag calculated from the bad stress profile does not validate the physical depth. The affected SLD flux partition, scalar-flux, and height panels and any fidelity comparisons derived from them must be withdrawn or visibly relabeled. The GABLS3 writer has the same future risk for its corresponding panels and the 03:00–04:00 UTC average.

Still interpretable as written: mean wind/thermodynamic/moisture profiles, native-face resolved fluxes and w²/w³, resolved TKE and its resolved transports, the actual boundary-condition wall stress/heat/moisture fluxes and friction velocity, low-level jet, SLD viscosity/diffusivity profiles, and filter/deficit/guard/cap/support series. `sgs_resolved_tke_dissipation` is already explicitly unavailable for SLD, not a physical zero. At heights where SLD coefficients are identically zero, the resolved-only flux equals the physical resolved-plus-SLD flux, but this does not validate the full profile or a first-crossing depth. The modeled trajectory was advanced with SLD's implicit solver; the diagnostic path did not alter its prognostic update.

## Recovery limit

The archived hourly Checkpointer files retain full 3-D `ρu`, `ρv`, thermodynamic/moisture densities, `Kᵘ`, tracer diffusivities, and SLD filter state. A read-only JLD2 inspection of the two-face GABLS1 final checkpoint found `simulation/model/clock.time=32400`, `simulation/model/fields/ρu/data` shape `(42,42,42)`, and `simulation/model/closure_fields/Kᵘ/data` shape `(42,42,43)`. These can support *exact constitutive flux snapshots at checkpoint times*, once the diagnostic formula and staggering/density conversion are validated against the pinned solver. They cannot recover exact 30-minute mean SGS profiles, minute-by-minute stress-height series, or exact GABLS3 12-sample 03:00–04:00 UTC flux averages. Reduced profiles save mean K and mean wind separately, not the 3-D/time correlation `<K ∂z u>`; closure filter series also lack local gradients. Multiplying mean K by mean shear is only an unvalidated approximation, not a corrected scientific export. Hourly checkpoints omit half-hour GABLS1 endpoints and most five-minute GABLS3 profile times. Exact complete recovery would require deterministic checkpoint replay with corrected writers (a bounded rerun), or new production; neither is authorized by this audit.
