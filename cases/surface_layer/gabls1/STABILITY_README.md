# Matched λ=1 stability-corrected SLD GABLS1 case

This case repeats the admitted filtered-wall, one-face, scheme-native SurfaceLayerDiffusivity (SLD) run with a single change: `stability_strength=1`. The closure divides the SLD viscosity by φₘ = 1 + λβₘζ and every scalar diffusivity by φₕ = 1 + λβₕζ, with βₘ = 4.8 and βₕ = 7.8 (the GABLS rough-wall slopes) and ζ = z/L at the first interior face (12.5 m). The local inverse Obukhov length 1/L = −κgF_θ/(θ₀u*³) is computed per column from the same filtered wall stress and filtered kinematic θ flux that drive SLD, with θ₀ = 263.5 K, the anelastic reference potential temperature. Columns with an upward or guarded heat flux keep φ = 1 and are counted as `stability_state` −1 or 0. The wall-exchange law, wall filter, grid, seed, advection, filter timescale, support and resolved-flux treatment are unchanged. The λ=0 control is the admitted `gabls1_n032_weno9_surface_layer_t300_s1_rf1p0_native_wallf300` run, which is not rerun.

`GABLS1_SLD_STABILITY_STRENGTH` relabels the case id with `_stabXpY`, and settings record λ, βₘ and βₕ. The `sld_stability` JLD2 output stores the local 1/L, state, face-1 φₘ and φₕ, and the filtered wall fluxes every 600 s. The reduced series add horizontal means of 1/L and face-1 φ, together with stable, neutral and upward-flux column fractions.

Admission requires everything the filtered-wall campaign required plus: finite 1/L and φ; φ ≥ 1 everywhere; a reported upward-flux column fraction; and CASE_DONE with a durable Slurm exit. Compare 7–8 h and 8–9 h with both the λ=0 control and the fixed 1 m LES median, but do not tune λ against that median. The comparison covers lowest-layer shear, w² and w³, wind and θ profiles, u*, heat flux, and the resolved, numerical and SGS flux partition.
